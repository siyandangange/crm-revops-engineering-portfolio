## Projects

### [01 | Consultation Lead Capture & CRM Integration](./01-consultation-lead-handoff/)
**HubSpot · Google Calendar · n8n · Salesforce · REST APIs · SOQL**

**Business problem:** Consultation enquiries captured in HubSpot need to reach Salesforce without manual re-entry or duplicate Lead creation.

**Solution:** Built and tested a workflow that retrieves HubSpot contacts, filters a test record, checks Salesforce for an existing Lead, and conditionally creates a new Lead.

**Results:** Successfully created a Salesforce Lead and prevented duplicate creation during a subsequent execution.

**Status:** Tested MVP. Automated booking-event detection remains future work.

### [02 | Job Intelligence Engine](./02-job-intelligence-engine/)
**Postman · JavaScript · External Job APIs · Salesforce · OAuth 2.0 / PKCE**

**Business problem:** Job opportunities from multiple sources are difficult to consolidate, standardize, score, and track.

**Solution:** Developed API requests, processing and scoring logic, and a Salesforce integration for importing structured job records.

**Results:** Successfully tested API requests and imported job data into Salesforce.

**Status:** In progress.

### 03 | Account Intelligence & Sales Research
**Clay · Account Research · Enrichment · Buying Signals**

**Business problem:** Sales teams need reliable account intelligence to prioritize opportunities and identify potential buying signals.

**Focus:** Researching how account data and enrichment can support lead qualification and sales prioritization.

**Status:** In progress.
